How to do Namaste
1
Bring both palms together at chest level, fingers pointing upward.
2
Give a slight bow of the head — deeper for elders.
3
Say "Namaste" clearly, with a genuine smile. No handshake needed.

Nepal in numbers

8,849m
Height of Everest
The world's tallest mountain was remeasured in 2020 and is now officially 8,848.86 m — Nepalis just round up.
8
of 10 tallest peaks
Nepal contains 8 of the world's 10 highest mountains, all above 8,000 metres — the "death zone".
50+
Festivals per year
Kathmandu Valley holds so many festivals that locals joke "we have more festivals than days in a year."
123
Languages spoken
Nepal's 30 million people speak 123 distinct languages — Nepali is the lingua franca but most have a mother tongue.
🐯
Tigers & rhinos live here
Chitwan National Park is one of the last homes of the one-horned rhinoceros and Bengal tiger in the wild.
1
Non-rectangular flag
Nepal is the only country in the world with a national flag that isn't a rectangle or square — it's two stacked triangles.
