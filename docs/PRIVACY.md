# Yatri Privacy Policy

_Last updated: July 5, 2026_

Yatri helps travelers avoid scams and access safety information in Nepal. Replace the bracketed operator/contact details and publish this policy at a public HTTPS URL before beta distribution.

## Data we process

- Account data: email address, display name, authentication identifiers, and travel preferences.
- Safety reports: report text, foreground location at submission time, timestamp, optional photo, and confirmations from other users.
- Saved data: offline districts and emergency contacts chosen by the user.
- Device location: requested only while Yatri is open, for nearby alerts, reports, and SOS messages. Yatri does not request background location.
- Safety check-ins: altitude symptom selections currently remain on the device and are not medical diagnoses.

## Why we process it

We use this data to authenticate users, provide offline packs, place and moderate community safety reports, reduce duplicate reports, prepare user-initiated SOS messages, prevent abuse, and maintain service security.

## Sharing

Community report text, approximate report location, time, trust status, and vote count may be visible to other users. Reporter email and saved emergency contacts are not public. Supabase processes hosted account and database data on our behalf. We do not sell personal data.

## Retention and control

Users can delete their account in Yatri. Account-linked profiles, reports, votes, saved districts, contacts, and report photos are deleted or scheduled for deletion through the backend. Locally downloaded data can be removed by deleting the app. Operational backups may expire on the hosting provider's backup schedule.

## Safety

Do not include passport numbers, payment-card data, medical records, or another person's private information in a report. Community reports are allegations until marked as verified by a moderator.
